The Studio

QuasarApp Group d.o.o. is a high-tech software and game development studio based in Bar, Montenegro.

We specialize in C++/Qt/QML engineering, cross-platform solutions and 3D engines - from system software and developer tools to our flagship 3D open-world title, Ecliptica.

The studio combines a large open-source footprint with B2B services for companies that need production-grade C++/Qt expertise.

Our mission is to build high-quality software that helps developers and organizations succeed.

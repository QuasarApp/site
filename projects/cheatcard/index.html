CheatCard

CheatCard - cross-platform loyalty and discount card application.

Desktop & Mobile Apps Released Open Source
Linux Windows Android macOS

The Problem

Loyalty and discount programs live on paper or in siloed apps, so small businesses struggle to run card programs and customers lose track of their benefits.

Why It Matters

CheatCard is a cross-platform loyalty and discount card application: customers carry every card in one place, and businesses run discount and loyalty programs without building custom software.

Features

  • Digital loyalty and discount cards in one wallet
  • Cross-platform Qt/QML client
  • Discount and loyalty program management
  • Built on the QuasarApp C++/Qt stack

Technology Stack

C++ Qt 6 QML SQLite

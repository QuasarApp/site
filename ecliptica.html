Ecliptica

Found Corporations. Fight for Resources. Build a Sustainable Future.

A first-person open-world colony survival simulator with a fully dynamic economy, built on our own Qt Quick 3D engine

Core Mechanics

Exploration & Living Landscapes

The planet holds traces of ancient eras, hidden in its massive and contrasting scenery: dense jungles, endless plains, and rocky highlands hiding rare resources and abandoned complexes of those who were here before you.

Interactive Preview

Community Economy & Delegation

Every survivor is an autonomous market participant. Formalize any task as a contract, or assign an NPC manager to run your facilities, hire staff, and adjust prices while you expand deeper into the planet.

Interactive Preview

Large-Scale Construction & Settlements

Vast open plains are ideal for large-scale construction and trade - but stretching your colony across the planet leaves it defenseless before the eyes of predators.

Interactive Preview

Found Corporations & Compete

Found your own corporation, fight for resources, and build a sustainable future - or be wiped out by ruthless nature, politics, and competition.
